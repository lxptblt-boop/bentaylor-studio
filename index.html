Lux Post Tenebras

Based in Maine, Ben works across surfaces and media — drawn to the symbolic and the totemic, imagery that carries weight without explanation. There is as much intention in what is removed as what is added. Things tend to look back.

Lux Post Tenebras — light after darkness — runs through the work as a quiet conviction: that the symbolic, the mortal, and the occasionally unsettling are not ends in themselves, but ways of moving through.

Diligent, experimental, hard to pin down.

Generally just having a good time.
